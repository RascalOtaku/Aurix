Short description:
A frontier saloon, ready to furnish: 15 low-poly props and an animated bartender, in 3D and pixel sprites.

Body:
Furnish the saloon from Frontier 1864, in 3D or 2D. Same style, palette and camera as the original pack.

Every prop comes two ways:
- 3D: .glb and .blend, low-poly, one simple material per part, vertex-color plank shading
- 2D: pixel sprites at 64 px and 96 px from 8 directions, outlined, with sprite sheets and JSON frame data
  (each sprite is framed to fill its cell, so set relative sizes in your engine)

Includes 15 props: bar counter with brass rail and spittoons, back bar with mirror and bottle shelves,
bar stool, chair, overturned chair, round card table, square poker table, upright piano with stool,
hanging oil lamp, wall sconce, whiskey crate, keg on a stand, coat rack, and a tileable wall section
and floor section to build the room.
Plus a bartender with an 8-frame walk, a 4-frame idle and an 8-frame bar-wiping loop, from all 8 directions.

Works in Godot, Unity, Unreal, Three.js, GameMaker and Tiled.

License: use in free and commercial games. Credit appreciated, not required. Don't resell the
assets on their own or as part of another asset pack.
