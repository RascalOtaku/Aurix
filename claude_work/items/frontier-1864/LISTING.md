Short description:
Low-poly 1860s frontier props as 3D models and ready-made pixel sprites.

Body:
Build a Colorado Territory boomtown, a mining camp or a trail across the plains, in 3D or 2D.

Every prop comes two ways:
- 3D: .glb and .blend, low-poly, flat-shaded, one simple material per part
- 2D: pixel sprites at 64 px and 96 px from 8 directions, outlined, with sprite sheets and JSON frame data
  (each sprite is framed to fill its cell, so set relative sizes in your engine)

Includes 21 props: whiskey barrel, supply crate, mine cart on rails, water trough, hitching post,
campfire (animated), boardwalk, saloon doors, wanted-poster board, rain barrel, hay bales,
mine entrance, ore pile, sluice box, miner's tent, dynamite crate, covered wagon, wagon wheel,
split-rail fence, telegraph pole, rock set. Plus a prospector with an 8-frame walk and 4-frame idle, from all 8 directions.

Works in Godot, Unity, Unreal, Three.js, GameMaker and Tiled.
