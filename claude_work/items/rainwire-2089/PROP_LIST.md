## UI kit (990+ files)
- Panels: window, terminal, chamfer and bracket frames in up to 6 colours, 9-slice
- Buttons: 5 colours x 4 states (normal, hover, pressed, disabled), plus icon buttons
- Bars and meters: bars at 0 to 100%, segmented bars, separate frames and fills, gauges, EQ, spinners
- HUD: corners, reticles, lock-on, dividers, chips, status labels, toggles, checkboxes, radios,
  slider, inputs, tooltips, toasts, tabs, scrollbars, radar sweep, badges, portrait frames, barcodes
- Icons: 73 icons in 7 colours
- Overlays: scanlines, grid, hex tile, noise, vignette, rain, glitch frames
- Assistant status art: 6 states as 160x80 faces, 72x16 strips and LED dot-matrix displays
- Font: Rainwire Mono pixel font (TTF + BMFont)
