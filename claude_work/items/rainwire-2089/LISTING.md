## Paid pack: Rainwire 2089, Neon Street Props + Cyberpunk UI Kit

Title: Rainwire 2089: Neon Street Props + Cyberpunk UI Kit

Short description:
20 detailed neon street props as 3D models and pixel sprites, plus a 990-piece cyberpunk UI kit.

Body:
Dress a rain-soaked neon city street, a back alley or a hacker's HUD, in 3D or 2D.

Every prop comes two ways:
- 3D: .glb and .blend, mid-poly (about 4k to 25k triangles each) with bevelled edges, smooth shading,
  panel lines, bolts and cables, and glowing neon parts on emissive materials
- 2D: pixel sprites at 64 px and 96 px from 8 directions, outlined, with sprite sheets and JSON frame data
  (each sprite is framed to fill its cell, so set relative sizes in your engine)

Includes 20 props: vending machine, neon bar sign, cargo crate, server rack (animated), hover drone (animated),
street ATM, dumpster, trash pile, wall AC units, street lamp, road barrier, satellite dish, LED traffic cone,
cable pole, steam vent (animated), noodle stall (animated), holo billboard (animated), sparking junction box
(animated), security camera (animated), OPEN 24 blade sign (animated).

The UI kit:
- Panels, buttons with 4 states, bars, gauges, segmented meters, toggles, sliders, inputs, tabs, tooltips, toasts
- HUD pieces: corners, reticles, lock-on, radar sweep, status labels, chips, portrait frames, barcodes
- 73 icons in 7 neon colours
- Overlays: scanlines, rain, grid, hex, noise, vignette, glitch frames
- Status art for a desktop AI assistant: 6 states (idle, listening, thinking, talking, focus, error)
  as faces, small strips and a dot-matrix LED display
- Rainwire Mono, an original pixel font (TTF + BMFont)
- PNG at 1x and 2x, crisp pixel versions, SVG, 9-slice margins in JSON, animation strips

Works in Godot, Unity, Unreal, Three.js, GameMaker, Tiled and any app that can show a PNG.

License: use in free and commercial games and apps. Credit appreciated, not required. Don't resell the
assets on their own or as part of another asset pack.

Updates: new props and UI pieces added free for everyone who bought.
